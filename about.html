About

What this is

A side project that puts every public, non-encrypted emergency and aviation audio feed for London, Ontario into one place.

The short version

Three feeds actually stream audio in your browser. Two more (CYXU tower and ground) open in a new tab on LiveATC because LiveATC blocks direct embedding. One channel is locked because it's encrypted. Everything else is documented with where it would come from.

What's included

  • London Fire + Public Works — Broadcastify feed 34296, community receiver on City of London OneVoice P25.
  • Middlesex County Fire Tac 1 + 3 — Broadcastify feed 18244, county-wide tactical channels.
  • Timmins Airport FSS + Local ATC — Broadcastify feed 35818, the only publicly streamable aviation feed within reasonable range.
  • CYXU Tower — 119.4 MHz, linked out to LiveATC (they block embedding).
  • CYXU Ground — 121.9 MHz, linked out to LiveATC.
  • Weatheradio London — reference only; no public internet stream, requires an SDR.

What's missing and why

  • London Police Service (LPS) dispatch — encrypted on the Ontario Public Safety Radio Network (PSRN). Cannot be legally received or streamed.
  • EMS dispatch — same: encrypted on PSRN since 2024.
  • OPP London detachment dispatch — also PSRN-encrypted; the previous non-encrypted OPP/MTO feed (31107) is no longer online.
  • Why LiveATC links out — LiveATC sits behind Cloudflare bot protection and explicitly prohibits hotlinking their streams. The right answer is "open in a new tab" — pretending we can proxy it would burn their bandwidth and break their TOS.

How it works technically

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no backend, no build step. For streamable channels, we embed the upstream provider's listen page in an iframe — that's the only cross-origin path that works for public-safety feeds (Broadcastify deprecated their 3rd-party embed API, and LiveATC sits behind Cloudflare bot protection). For channels that can't be embedded (LiveATC, encrypted), we show a clear CTA card with a deep link. Leaflet renders the coverage map. That's it.

Legal and ethical notes

Listening to public-safety radio in Canada is legal. Decrypting encrypted channels is not, and we don't attempt to. All streams are linked or embedded with attribution to the original broadcasters — we don't proxy their bytes, we don't strip their ads, we don't claim the feeds. This site is not a substitute for emergency services. In an emergency, dial 911.
